# Privacy Notice

I Renovation (the platform) is an AI renovation design and contractor-matching service. This notice explains how we collect, use, keep and delete your personal information. Registering means you have read and accepted it (we record which version you accepted, and when).

## What we collect

- **Account:** mobile number, display name, your chosen language.
- **What you upload:** floor plan images, room photos, documents, and the text you give us in chat.
- **Requirements and project:** the home's city, district, floor area, layout, renovation type, style, budget range, desired start and description. Phone numbers and email addresses are not allowed in project text; we keep your contact details separately.
- **Records of the work:** quotes, contract, change orders, construction and acceptance records, reviews, and the conversation with our AI agents.
- **What we don't collect:** we ask only for what each stage needs (for a child, their age, never their name). The location data (GPS) in photos is removed before we store them.

## How we use it

Only to run the service: requirements, design, matching contractors, comparing quotes, the contract and construction follow-up, acceptance, after-sales and reminders; and to produce your documents (requirement sheet, reports, quote sheets, acceptance lists) and our internal records.

## AI processing

To do this work, the floor plans, photos, documents and project text you give us are sent to the Xiaomi MiMo API for our AI agents to work on. It is the only closed outside service we use (you have agreed to this). Please don't upload sensitive material unrelated to the renovation. Every AI-made image is labelled "AI-generated".

## Who can see it

- **Matched contractor teams:** see only a brief of the project inside your invitation (city, district, floor area, layout, renovation type, style, budget range, start date, description, and the confirmed design package if there is one) — never your name, contact or exact address. Only the team you pick gets your name and phone.
- **The team you pick:** receives your name and phone, for the site visit and the contract.
- **Your Homebox handover record:** it is your own household, under your control.
- **MiMo (Xiaomi's model API):** as described above, processes the material you give us for the renovation.

## How long we keep it

- While your account is active; files are deleted a set time after your last project ends (proposal: 12 months).
- The contract, change orders and acceptance records stay until the warranty ends, because both sides may need them.
- A contractor's documents are deleted a set time after they leave (proposal: 12 months).
- Backups age out after 30 days.

## Your rights

- Ask for a copy of everything we hold about you.
- Ask for deletion: after we confirm, we delete your account and records (records both sides of a project need stay until the warranty ends), including the Homebox household; backups age out as above.
- Correct your details, stop reminders, at any time.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; where a renovation concerns a child we only take what a stage needs, such as their age.

## Changes

New versions are shown at registration and sign-in; using the service after that means you accept them, and we ask you to confirm again for important changes.

## Contact

Through the chat on the platform, or the channel you chose. This notice is not legal advice; the renovation contract is between the homeowner and the contractor, and the platform is not a party to it.
