These terms apply to every registered user of I Renovation (homeowners and contractors). Registering means you accept them (we record the version and the time). Contractors must also accept the Contractor Terms.

## What the platform does, and does not do

- We provide: AI renovation design, contractor matching, quote comparison, and the records and reminders from requirements to after-sales.
- We are not a party to the renovation contract: it is signed between the homeowner and the contractor. We do not do building work, give legal advice, or certify safety — structural, gas and electrical work is signed off by people licensed for it.
- We never hold or pass on money: the homeowner pays the contractor directly at the milestones in the contract.
- In a dispute, both sides get the same records and we help them talk; liability is settled by the contract and the law.

## Accounts

- Register with a mobile number and a password; keep your password safe. If you forget it, we reset it after checking it is you.

## What both sides must do

- No harassment, and no going around the platform to ask for someone's contact details.
- AI-made images are labelled "AI-generated"; 3D views and renders show a design, not the finished home.

Design work (the renovation report, the design package) is paid; prices and the refund rule are what the page says. Payments are confirmed by hand.
