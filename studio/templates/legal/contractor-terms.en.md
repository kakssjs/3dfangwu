# Contractor Terms

These terms apply to contractor users of I Renovation (renovation companies and construction teams). They are accepted together with the Terms of Use and the Privacy Notice (we record the version and the time).

## Joining and verification

- Register a contractor account in the name of your company or team, and send: the company or team name, the 18-character credit code on your business licence (营业执照), the construction qualifications you claim, your city, districts, renovation types, the styles you work in, your usual price per m², years in business, team size, how many projects you can take at once, a description, and at least 3 real past projects with photos.
- We verify each one: the licence is checked on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (国家企业信用信息公示系统), and the qualifications and projects are reviewed by hand. Only verified contractors can be matched or invited.
- Changing a fact we checked (name, licence number, qualifications, city, years, past projects) sends you back to review.
- Fake material (licence, qualifications, projects) means removal.

## Matching and quotes

- Matching follows rules anyone can read: work in the project's city and district, offer its kind of renovation, then a fit tier with reasons from your price band, styles, years in business and past projects in the district. There is no paid ranking, and no place in a shortlist can be bought.
- A project invites at most 3 teams at once; you can be invited once per project, and no invitation reaches you while your open ones already equal the projects you said you can take at once.
- Within 7 days of an invitation: decline (a reason helps), or give one sealed quote. Quotes use our form only (with a design package, the standard quote sheet); you can revise it until the homeowner picks.
- No team sees another team's quote, the other invited teams, or who else was invited.

## Conduct

- Quote on real projects only; no invented past projects or reviews.
- Until a homeowner picks you, do not contact them or ask anyone for their contact details; the platform exchanges contacts after the pick.
- No price changes after quoting, except through a change order the homeowner approves.
- No harassment, no advertising unrelated to the renovation.

## Records and reviews

- Your project record (on time, phases passed first time, complaints upheld) and reviews from projects finished on the platform go on your profile; a score starts once there are enough finished projects.

## Fees

- Joining and taking work is free during the event. Afterwards a fee per won project applies; the standard will be published and agreed separately.

## Warnings, suspension and removal

- Breaking these terms leads to: a warning, suspension (no matching, invitations or quotes; work under way continues), or removal, decided by us; homeowners of projects under way are told.

## Liability

- The platform provides design, matching and records and is not a party to the renovation contract; the quality and safety of the work are the contractor's responsibility, and structural, gas and electrical work is signed off by people licensed for it.
- In a dispute both sides get the same records and we help them talk; liability is settled by the contract and the law.

## Changes and language

New versions are published; using the service after that means you accept them. These terms come in Chinese and English; if they differ, the Chinese text is the one that counts.
